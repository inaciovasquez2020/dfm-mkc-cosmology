\section{Proposed reproduction specification}
The assumed potential and initial matter contents were reported as
\[
U(\phi)=\frac12\phi^2+\frac14\phi^4,\qquad
\phi(0)=1,\quad \dot\phi(0)=0,
\]
\[
\rho_m(0)=0.1,\qquad \rho_r(0)=0.1,\qquad H(0)=0.
\]
The reported initial density is $\rho_0=0.95$ in the chosen units. For
curvature $k=+1$ and $G=1$, the constraint-based initial scale factor would be
\[
a_0=\sqrt{\frac{3}{8\pi G\rho_0}}
=0.3544695181296627\ldots
\]
provided the stated Friedmann convention and parameters are the intended ones.

The proposed first-order system is
\[
\dot a=aH,\qquad \dot\phi=v,\qquad
\dot v=-3Hv-U'(\phi),
\]
\[
\dot\rho_m=-3H\rho_m,\qquad
\dot\rho_r=-4H\rho_r,
\]
\[
\rho=\frac12v^2+U(\phi)+\rho_m+\rho_r,\qquad
p=\frac12v^2-U(\phi)+\frac13\rho_r,
\]
\[
\dot H=-4\pi G(\rho+p)+\frac{1}{a^2},\qquad
H^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho-\frac{1}{a^2}.
\]
This is a proposed reproduction specification, not a verified execution
record. A valid reproduction must retain the exact initial constraint,
solver versions and tolerances, event definitions, raw event output, and
constraint residuals throughout integration.
